## WordPerfect vs Microsoft Word: Pros and Cons

**WordPerfect pros:** Reveal Codes for direct control of formatting; strong fit for legal and legacy document workflows; long-standing template and macro support.

**WordPerfect cons:** Smaller ecosystem and weaker real-time collaboration; DOCX exchange can introduce formatting shifts; fewer people know it, which matters when documents move between teams.

**Microsoft Word pros:** DOCX is the industry standard; real-time collaboration and cloud storage through Microsoft 365; available on Windows, macOS, web and mobile.

**Microsoft Word cons:** Subscription pricing for most buyers; less transparent formatting control than Reveal Codes.

## Who Should Choose WordPerfect?

Choose WordPerfect if your office has years of WPD templates and macros, your team already uses Reveal Codes daily, and your documents rarely need to be edited by outside collaborators. ## Who Should Choose Microsoft Word?

Choose Word if documents circulate among clients, courts and teams using Microsoft 365, if you need real-time co-authoring, or if you are starting fresh without legacy WordPerfect files.
