## What Is GearUP Booster?

GearUP Booster is a game-optimized network accelerator (often called a "gaming VPN" though it's more precisely a traffic routing optimizer). It intercepts your game's UDP/TCP traffic and routes it through GearUP's global server network to find the lowest-latency path to the game server — bypassing suboptimal ISP routing that can add unnecessary hops and latency.

It's designed for players who experience high ping, packet loss, or jitter — especially those playing on foreign servers (playing NA servers from Europe or Asia, for example) or those with ISPs known for poor peering agreements.

## Does It Actually Work? How to Test It Yourself

Results depend on your ISP, location, game server and time of day. GearUP's value is generally greatest for cross-region play (for example, connecting to servers on another continent) or on ISPs with poor routing. For same-region play with already-good routing, expect little or no benefit, and sometimes a few extra milliseconds.

To measure it on your setup, use the free trial:

1. Note your baseline ping and packet loss in your game (or with a ping/traceroute tool) at peak hours.
2. Enable GearUP for the same game server and repeat the measurement.
3. Compare average ping, jitter and packet loss across several sessions, not just one.
4. Keep it only if the improvement is clear on the servers you actually play.

## Packet Loss and Jitter

GearUP markets itself mainly on reducing packet loss and jitter by routing traffic through its server network, and this is where such tools tend to help most on congested or poorly peered connections. Even 1–2% packet loss causes stuttering and desync in competitive games, so compare packet loss with and without GearUP during your trial rather than relying on ping alone.

## Key Features

### Smart Route Selection
GearUP runs a route analysis at game launch, testing multiple paths through its server network and selecting the lowest-latency path. It re-evaluates routes every 90 seconds during gameplay.

### Auto-Boost
Detects when you launch a supported game and automatically activates — no manual game selection needed for popular titles.

### 2,000+ Game Support
Supports virtually every major online game including MMOs, battle royales, MOBAs, FPS games, and mobile games.

### Node Selection
Manual node selection lets you specify which GearUP server to route through — useful if you want to guarantee a specific regional path.
