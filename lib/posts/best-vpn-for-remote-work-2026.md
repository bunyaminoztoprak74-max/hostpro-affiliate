## Remote-Work Security Checklist

- Use the employer-provided connection method.
- Enable phishing-resistant MFA where available.
- Keep full-disk encryption and automatic updates enabled.
- Separate work and personal browser profiles.
- Avoid sensitive calls or screens in public spaces.
- Verify the VPN kill switch before travel.
- Keep recovery codes offline and secure.

## Consumer VPN vs. Corporate Access: Which Do Remote Workers Actually Need?

Remote workers often confuse **traffic privacy on public Wi-Fi** with **authenticated access to private company systems**:

- **Use a consumer VPN (NordVPN, Surfshark, Proton VPN)** when you work from cafés, hotels, airports, or coworking spaces and want encrypted transit on untrusted networks (provided your employer's IT policy permits consumer VPN software).
- **Use a corporate VPN or Zero Trust tool (Tailscale, Cloudflare Zero Trust)** when IT teams need identity-aware access to staging servers, internal dashboards, databases, or office subnets without exposing ports to the public internet.
- **Pair your VPN with credential hygiene:** Encrypted transit does not protect against reused passwords or phishing. Combine your VPN with a dedicated [password manager](/blog/best-password-manager-2026) and phishing-resistant MFA.
