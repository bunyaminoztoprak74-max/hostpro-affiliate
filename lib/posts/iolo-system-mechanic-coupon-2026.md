## Avoid the Wrong Bundle

Iolo markets more than one System Mechanic package. A basic optimization product and a broader security/privacy bundle are not interchangeable. Check whether the cart includes security, password, privacy or recovery tools that you already receive elsewhere.

The site currently promotes System Mechanic Ultimate Defense as a broader Windows optimization and security suite, but offers can change without notice. If a code is supplied, confirm it visibly changes the final total before payment.

## Introductory Price vs Renewal Price

A large first-term discount can become expensive if the subscription renews automatically at a higher amount. Iolo's official page states that automatic renewal may be enabled during checkout and preferences can be updated through the customer account.

Before paying:

- Save the checkout terms.
- Add the renewal date to your calendar.
- Confirm where renewal can be disabled.
- Re-evaluate the product before renewal.
- Keep the order email and account login.

Do not rely on a coupon site's wording when the merchant checkout shows different terms.

## How to Test a Coupon Safely
