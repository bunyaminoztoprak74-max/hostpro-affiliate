Hosting review platforms can help you spot patterns, but they need to be read critically. *Editorial note: HostPro Reviews has not run its own lab tests for this guide. Figures below are arithmetic, vendor-stated, or attributed to a named source.*

## Where hosting ratings come from

- **Trustpilot, G2, Capterra:** customer-submitted reviews. Unhappy customers are more likely to post, and some hosts actively solicit positive reviews, so look at recent reviews and the pattern of complaints rather than the headline score.
- **Reddit and forums:** useful for candid experiences, but anecdotal.
- **Editorial review sites:** opinions based on methodology that varies widely. Check whether the site discloses affiliate relationships and says how it evaluated each host.

## How to read a review score

- Look at the number of reviews and how recent they are.
- Read the most common complaints. Billing, renewal price and support responsiveness come up often for hosts of all sizes.
- Separate complaints about the host from complaints about a plugin, theme or the reviewer's own setup.
- Check whether the review is verified and whether the host replies.

## Common complaints worth checking before you buy

- Renewal price increases after the intro term.
- Upsells at checkout.
- Resource limits on shared plans.
- Support that pushes upgrades instead of fixing the problem.
