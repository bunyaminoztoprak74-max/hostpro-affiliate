A good hosting decision weighs several criteria, not one headline number. This is a checklist you can use to score hosts yourself. *Editorial note: HostPro Reviews has not run its own lab tests for this guide. Figures below are arithmetic, vendor-stated, or attributed to a named source.*

## Ten criteria worth scoring

1. **Pricing:** intro price and renewal price, and the billing term required.
2. **Performance features:** caching, CDN, NVMe storage, supported PHP versions.
3. **Reliability commitments:** uptime SLA, remedy, exclusions and a public status page.
4. **Support:** channels, hours, scope and escalation.
5. **WordPress tooling:** staging, backups, one-click updates, rollback.
6. **Security:** SSL, firewall, malware scanning, backups.
7. **Scalability:** how easily you can upgrade as traffic grows.
8. **Resource limits:** CPU, memory, inodes and visit allowances.
9. **Policies:** money-back window, migration help and email hosting.
10. **Fit:** whether the host matches your skills and site type.

## How to use it

Weight the criteria by what matters to your site, score each host from its published plan details, then verify performance and support during the money-back window. Ratings elsewhere on this site are editorial opinions, not lab scores.
