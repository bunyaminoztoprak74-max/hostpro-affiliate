## Pros and Cons of Buying During a Sale

**Pros:**
- Lower entry cost to test a host risk-free
- Often bundled with a free domain or SSL
- Money-back guarantee windows still apply

**Cons:**
- Renewal price shock is common — budget for year two
- Long lock-in terms reduce flexibility if the host underperforms
- Some "deals" are simply the everyday list price

## Conclusion
