NordPass and LastPass both generate, store and autofill credentials across common devices. The deciding issue is not a single feature checkbox: it is how much weight you give product history, recovery design, account administration and your own migration risk.

## Security Model

Both products use a zero-knowledge design intended to keep vault contents unreadable to the provider. That does not eliminate risk. Weak master passwords, malicious browser extensions, stolen sessions and exposed recovery methods can bypass otherwise strong encryption. Use a unique, long master passphrase and phishing-resistant MFA.

## Migration Safety

Exports are commonly unencrypted CSV files. Complete the move on a trusted, patched computer; import immediately; verify a sample of records; then securely remove the export and empty synced recycle bins. Rotate the most sensitive credentials after migration.

## How to Evaluate the Security History

LastPass has publicly documented its 2022 security incident and subsequent security changes. Buyers should read the provider's incident material directly and decide whether current architecture, operational controls and transparency meet their risk tolerance.

NordPass avoiding the same incident history does not make any account automatically safe. A compromised email account, weak master passphrase, malicious extension or exposed recovery method can still undermine the vault.

For either product:

1. Use a unique master passphrase.
2. Enable the strongest supported MFA.
3. Protect the account email with separate MFA.
4. Store recovery material offline.
5. Remove old devices and browser sessions.
6. Keep a tested export and exit plan.

## Family and Business Considerations

Families should test shared items, emergency recovery and what happens when the organizer loses access. Businesses should prioritize identity provisioning, audit logs, offboarding, policy enforcement and administrator recovery over a small consumer discount.

Run a pilot with several non-critical accounts. Test desktop, browser and phone autofill, then complete a recovery exercise before importing the full vault.
