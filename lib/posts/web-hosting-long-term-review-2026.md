Hosting quality can change after the promotional period. *Editorial note: HostPro Reviews has not run its own lab tests for this guide. Figures below are arithmetic, vendor-stated, or attributed to a named source.*

## What can change after sign-up

- **Renewal pricing:** promotional rates usually apply to the first term; renewals are typically higher. Always check the renewal price.
- **Resource limits:** shared plans have limits on CPU, memory and processes, and some hosts enforce them more strictly as your site grows.
- **Server load:** over-sold shared servers can slow down over time. Slowdowns may show up at peak hours.
- **Plan and feature changes:** hosts revise plans, limits and policies.
- **Support quality:** it can vary with staffing and ticket volume.

## How to track a host over time

1. Run a free uptime monitor from day one and keep the history.
2. Re-test speed monthly with the same tool, page and location so results are comparable.
3. Record support interactions and outcomes.
4. Review your renewal date and price well in advance.

## Questions to ask before renewing

- What is the renewal price, and is there a multi-year option?
- Have my resource limits or plan features changed?
- Is my site still fast on an uncached page, not only a cached one?
- Would a different plan or a managed platform suit my traffic now?
