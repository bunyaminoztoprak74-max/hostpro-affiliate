Speed claims are not useful without price context. This guide groups hosting options by budget and explains what drives speed. *Editorial note: HostPro Reviews has not run its own lab tests for this guide. Figures below are arithmetic, vendor-stated, or attributed to a named source.*

## What makes a host faster

### Storage: NVMe vs SSD vs HDD
NVMe drives generally offer higher throughput and lower latency than SATA SSDs, and both are faster than hard disks.

### Web server software
Apache, Nginx and LiteSpeed handle concurrency differently. LiteSpeed-based hosts commonly bundle a server-level cache plugin.

### Server-side caching
Cached pages skip PHP and database work, so they are usually much faster than uncached ones.

## By budget tier

- **Under about $5/month:** budget shared hosting with caching and a CDN. Check renewal pricing, since promotional rates apply to the first term.
- **About $5-20/month:** higher-tier shared plans and entry managed or cloud plans, which may add staging and better support.
- **Premium managed WordPress:** managed platforms built around WordPress, typically with higher prices and more included tooling.

## Speed and your site

Page speed affects user experience and is part of Google's page experience signals, but it is one factor among many. The effect on conversions varies by site, so measure your own before paying for a premium plan.

## Where to find independent benchmarks

To check speed for your own site, test a fresh install during the money-back window from your visitors' regions.
